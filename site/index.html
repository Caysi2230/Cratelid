No. NEREUS Lid upgrades the crate you already own. The crate and e-bike are not included.

No. NEREUS Lid is designed initially for Basil and Bang Bang e-bike crates, and initial compatibility is being finalised. Tell us your crate model when you join the first drop.

NEREUS Lid is intended to provide weather-resistant protection. The hard-shell lid and integrated liner are designed to help keep rain, spray, sand and grit away from your gear. It carries no IP rating and isn’t designed to be submerged.
