How it works

One lid. One liner.
Nothing else to fit.

  1. Step 1

    Keep your existing crate

    Already riding with a rear crate? Good. That stays.

  2. Step 2

    Clip NEREUS Lid onto it

    The lid clips onto the crate rim. Closed, it’s a lockable hard-shell top.

  3. Step 3

    Open it up

    The flexible liner drops into the crate. The organiser sits under the lid.

Integrated liner

Keeps water out. Gear in.

The flexible liner is attached to the underside of the lid. Open NEREUS and it drops into your crate, lining the base and sides to cover the crate’s holes and help keep water, sand and grit away from your gear.

  • Attached to the lid, so there’s nothing extra to fit
  • Lines the base and all four sides
  • Flexible, so your full crate space stays usable

Under-lid organiser

A place for the small stuff.

A slim organiser under the lid keeps the essentials where you can find them, so the main crate stays open for the big stuff.

  • Wax
  • Sunscreen
  • Keys
  • Fin key

Top straps

Carry your wetsuit home.

Two grey straps across the top secure a rolled wetsuit, towel or other soft gear. The wet stuff rides on top. The dry stuff stays locked inside.

FAQ

Does the crate come with it?

No. NEREUS Lid upgrades the crate you already own. The crate and e-bike are not included.

Will it fit every crate?

No. NEREUS Lid is designed initially for selected popular e-bike crate styles, and initial compatibility is being finalised. Tell us your crate model when you join the first drop.

Is it waterproof?

NEREUS Lid is intended to provide weather-resistant protection. The hard-shell lid and integrated liner are designed to help keep rain, spray, sand and grit away from your gear. It carries no IP rating and isn’t designed to be submerged.

Is this available now?

Not yet. NEREUS is preparing its first production run. Join the first drop and you’ll be among the first to know when it opens.
